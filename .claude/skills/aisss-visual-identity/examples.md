# Examples

Worked layouts. Each one uses the rules in `SKILL.md` and the reference pages; nothing here adds a rule.

## Title card [PDF p.13]

Input: a 1920×1080 card for the reward-hacking explainer.

Do this:

- Ground: Ink `#111111`, full frame, square.
- Label, top left, inside an 8% margin: `reward hacking` in Inter Bold small caps, tracked, Paper.
- Headline: `The model found a shortcut.` Baskerville Bold, Paper, sentence case, left aligned. Not Inter.
- Deck: `A short explainer.` Inter Regular, grey no lighter than `#C8C8C8` on Ink.
- One Strand loop (`strand-drift.svg`) in `#C64D0A`, ends off the right and bottom edges. The headline is Paper, so the loop is Strand.
- White lockup, bottom left, about 280px wide, with clear space the height of the yarn mark. The loop does not enter that box.

Do not add a gradient, a subtitle in Strand, or a second loop.

## Lower third [PDF p.13]

Input: a name strap for the same film.

- Bar: Wool `#E3D5C3`, square ends, about 88px tall.
- Left edge: 4px Strand rule, full height of the bar.
- Label: `02 · What good looks like`, Ink, Inter Bold small caps.
- Title: `Reward hacking`, Ink, Baskerville Bold, sentence case.
- Black yarn mark at the right, 36px, inside the bar's padding.

## Document top [PDF p.13]

- Ground: Warm Paper `#F4EFE6`. Lockup at the top in Ink.
- Label: `briefing note`, Inter Bold small caps.
- Title: `Lower the barrier, and the idea travels.` Baskerville Bold, Ink.
- Under the title: one Strand rule, 1.5–2pt. It is the only Strand on the page.
- Body: Ink on Warm Paper, Inter Regular, left aligned, 60–75 characters a line.

## Hero line [PDF p.11]

Input: the site hero, or a slide that quotes it. Set, in this order:

1. `Complex AI safety topics, ` in Redaction 35 Regular, Ink, tracking −0.035em.
2. `said simply` in Inter Medium, Strand `#C64D0A`.
3. `.` in Ink.

If Redaction is unavailable, step 1 is Inter Regular. Do not draw bars by hand.

## A diagram [PDF p.8]

- Ground Warm Paper or Paper. Structure in Ink. **One** signal colour for the one thing that matters: Indigo for structure, or Moss for the confirmed point. Saffron only as a tick or a short underline.
- Labels: Inter, Paper on Indigo/Moss fields (both AAA) or Ink on the ground.
- No Strand on a line that already uses a signal.

## What to reject

- A thumbnail with the wordmark in orange, a rounded white card, a headline in Inter instead of Baskerville Bold, and three decorative loops is off-system even if the yarn is recognisable. Rebuild it as Ink type on Paper, the SVG lockup in one colour, a Baskerville headline, and no loop at all if the frame is already busy.
- Strand body copy, Live as text on Paper, a logo on a photograph, Warm Paper beside Paper, a stacked lockup, a drop shadow on a card: all off-system.

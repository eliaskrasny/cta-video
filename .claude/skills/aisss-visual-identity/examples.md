# Examples

## Title card

Input: a 1920×1080 card for the reward-hacking explainer.

Do this:

- Ground: Ink `#111111`, full frame, square.
- Label, top left, inside an 8% margin: `reward hacking` in Inter Bold small caps, tracking 0.14em, Paper.
- Headline: `The model found a shortcut.` Baskerville Bold, Paper, left aligned. Not Inter.
- One Strand loop (`strand-drift.svg`) in `#C64D0A`, ends off the right and bottom edges. The headline is Paper, so the loop is Strand.
- White lockup, bottom left, width about 280px, with clear space the height of the yarn mark. The loop does not enter that box.

Do not add a gradient, a subtitle in Strand, or a second loop.

## Lower third

Input: a name strap for the same film.

- Bar: Wool `#E3D5C3`, square ends, about 88px tall.
- Left edge: 4px Strand rule, full height of the bar.
- Label: `02 · What good looks like`, Ink, Inter Bold small caps, tracking 0.12em.
- Title: `Reward hacking`, Ink, Baskerville Bold, sentence case.
- Black yarn mark at the right, 36px, inside the bar’s padding.

## Hero line

Input: the site hero, or a slide that quotes it.

Set, in this order:

1. `Complex AI safety topics, ` in Redaction 35 Regular, Ink, tracking −0.035em.
2. `said simply` in Inter, Strand `#C64D0A`.
3. `.` in Ink.

If Redaction is unavailable, step 1 is Inter Regular. Do not draw bars by hand.

## What to reject

A thumbnail with the wordmark in orange, a rounded white card, an Inter headline, and three decorative loops is off-system even if the yarn is recognizable. Rebuild it as Ink type on Paper, the SVG lockup in one color, and no loop at all if the frame is already busy.

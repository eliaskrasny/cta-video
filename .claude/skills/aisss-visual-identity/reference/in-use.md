## Production notes (carried over from the earlier skill; not printed in the PDF)

### Video
- **Title card:** Ink field, Inter small-cap label, Baskerville Bold title in Paper, optional one-line deck in a grey no lighter than `#C8C8C8` on Ink, white lockup inside clear space, one Strand loop, square frame.
- **Lower third:** Wool `#E3D5C3`, 4px Strand bar at the left edge, Ink label and title, black mark at the right. No outline.
- **Captions:** Inter, Ink on Paper or Paper on Ink. Never Strand, Live or Saffron.
- **End card:** Ink, white lockup, `aisafetysaidsimply.com`, optional `strand-corner.svg` in Strand, kept clear of the lockup.
- Keep type inside an 8% title-safe margin. Logo clear space still applies inside it.
- Don't place the logo on a moving image without a solid field.

### Documents
- Ground is Paper or Warm Paper, not both. Lockup at the top; one Strand rule under the title.
- Body: Ink on the ground in Inter Regular, left aligned, about 60–75 characters a line.

### Cards and UI
- Cards are square with no shadow. Live cards use `#FF8A3D` with Ink type. Inactive cards use Paper 3.
- Primary button: Ink fill, Paper type. Secondary: Paper 2 fill, Ink type. Focus ring stays Ink.

# Real-story images

Evidence that the story is real: screenshots of news articles, posts, documents and web pages, and photos of the real people and events in the video.

**Name:** `<yyyy-mm-dd>-<publisher>-<slug>.png`, using the date it was published, lower case with hyphens, e.g. `2025-09-29-latimes-sb53-signed.png`, `2024-03-11-encode-sneha-revanur.jpg`.

**Add a row to `credits.csv`:**

| Column | Example |
|---|---|
| file | 2025-09-29-latimes-sb53-signed.png |
| kind | article (or post, document, page, photo) |
| publisher | Los Angeles Times |
| title | Headline, post or photo subject |
| date | 2025-09-29 (published) |
| url | https://… |
| permission | fair use: news commentary (or "permission from <name>", or the photo's licence) |
| added_by | your initials |

**Taking a screenshot**
- Desktop browser at 100% zoom, light mode, window about 1600 px wide.
- Capture the headline, the publisher's name and date, and the first paragraph. Crop out ads, cookie banners, sign-up pop-ups and the browser bar.
- Never edit, retype or rearrange the words. If the article has been updated, capture the version you're quoting.
- Don't capture text behind a paywall that you couldn't read yourself.
- Leave out the names and faces of private people who aren't part of the story (crop them out; don't blur).

**Photos of real people:** only with their permission or a licence that allows it, and always credited. Never edited beyond cropping.
